Connect your repositories

Mothership gives you leverage: engineering judgment written down once and continuously applied to every repository you connect, instead of re-reviewing each project by hand. It's deliberately safety-railed - dry-run by default, rate-capped, every finding validated before anything is ever filed - because trusting an AI reviewer to act autonomously has to be earned, not assumed.

  1. Install the GitHub App on the org/repos you want reviewed.
  2. Complete a short checkout to activate your subscription.
  3. You're done - Mothership starts covering your connected repos automatically.
